# PhD in Business Administration Online | GAMI Singapore

Looking to advance your academic and professional journey with a **PhD in Business Administration Online**? This repository provides curated resources, insights, and trusted pathways for working professionals and aspiring researchers.

## 🎓 About the Programme

A PhD in Business Administration Online is a research-focused doctoral degree designed for individuals aiming to contribute to academic knowledge and pursue careers in research, teaching, or high-level consulting.

Unlike a DBA, this programme emphasizes:
- Original research and thesis development  
- Scholarly publications  
- Academic and research-oriented career paths
